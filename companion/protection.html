Scope and undo

Destination blocking covers the tested top-level host and its subdomains on all ports and schemes. For requests without a top-level page, the initiating host is used. Location is denied at the exact tested address, including when embedded elsewhere; embedded pages cannot bypass that denial. A lab result does not guarantee every everyday interaction will work.

Pause stops a site's controls and keeps its profile and alias. Undo removes them. Existing browser preferences return when Ariadne's location rules are removed. Reload pages after changing controls; undo cannot recall information already sent.

Profile integrity checks consistency. The receipt is not a signature or independent proof of the original captures. Approximate location remains lab-only.
